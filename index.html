      How to play

      The basics

      Match the top card of the pile by colour, by number, or by symbol. Wild cards go on anything. If nothing fits, take a card from the deck — you may play it straight away if it matches.

      Play your second-to-last card and hit UNO!. If you forget, any other player can hit Catch! before the next player moves, and you draw two.

      Action cards

      • Skip — the next player misses their turn.
      • Reverse — turn order flips (a Skip with two players).
      • Draw Two / Four — next player draws and is skipped.
      • Wild — you choose the colour in play.

      A Wild Draw is only allowed when you hold no card of the colour in play. The next player may challenge it: a bluffer takes the cards instead, but a wrong challenge costs the challenger two extra.

      If the first card turned up is an action card, it acts on the first player.

      UNO FLIP!

      Every card has a Light face and a Dark face. A Flip card turns the deck, the pile and every hand over to the Dark Side — the cards you were holding become something else entirely.

      UNO Show 'Em No Mercy

      A 168-card deck with official rules that are much harsher:

      • No match? Keep drawing until a card fits, then play it.
      • Stacking — answer a +2, +4, +6 or +10 with a draw card of equal or higher value (any colour). The penalty adds up, and whoever cannot stack takes all of it.
      • 7 — swap hands with a player of your choice. 0 — everyone passes their hand on.
      • Discard All — throw down every card of that colour.
      • Skip Everyone — you go again.
      • Wild Reverse Draw 4 — reverse, then the next player draws 4 (with two players it comes back at you).
      • Wild Draw 6 / 10 — the next player draws 6 or 10.
      • Colour Roulette — the next player names a colour and draws until one turns up.
      • Mercy rule — 25 cards in your hand and you are out.

      Go out first, or be the last player standing, to win the hand. The winner scores the other hands (colour actions 20, wild actions 50) plus 250 for every player knocked out.

      Scoring

      House rule for Classic and FLIP: the round carries on after someone goes out, until only one player is left holding cards — that player loses.

      The first player out scores what is left in every other hand: numbers at face value, Light actions 20, Dark actions 30, Wild 40, Wild Draw Two/Four 50, Wild Draw Colour 60.
