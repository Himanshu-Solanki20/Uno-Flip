      How to play

      The basics

      Match the top card of the pile by colour, by number, or by symbol. Wild cards go on anything. If nothing fits, take a card from the deck — you may play it straight away if it matches.

      Play your second-to-last card and hit UNO!. If you forget, any other player can hit Catch! before the next player moves, and you draw two.

      Action cards

      • Skip — the next player misses their turn.
      • Reverse — turn order flips (a Skip with two players).
      • Draw Two / Four — next player draws and is skipped.
      • Wild — you choose the colour in play.

      A Wild Draw is only allowed when you hold no card of the colour in play. The next player may challenge it: a bluffer takes the cards instead, but a wrong challenge costs the challenger two extra.

      If the first card turned up is an action card, it acts on the first player.

      UNO FLIP!

      Every card has a Light face and a Dark face. A Flip card turns the deck, the pile and every hand over to the Dark Side — the cards you were holding become something else entirely.

      Scoring

      The winner scores what is left in every other hand: numbers at face value, Light actions 20, Dark actions 30, Wild 40, Wild Draw Two/Four 50, Wild Draw Colour 60.
